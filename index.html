Gary Calendar

Gary Calendar is a private, internal tool used by Jorgensen Holdings to manage its own Google Calendar and Gmail for rental property scheduling, such as booking property showings and reading showing requests.

It is used only by Jorgensen Holdings for its own account. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's Google data.
